Privacy Statement: Broccoli Buttons & EventWhisperer

Effective Date: 2026-11-18 · Contact: broccolibuttons@gmail.com

In plain words: This website does not track visitors or show ads. In EventWhisperer, your calendar stays on your phone or tablet and is never sent to our servers or AI services. If you choose to sign in with Google, that token stays encrypted on your device. Test and release builds only send cleaned crash reports (without your email, login keys, or calendar events) that are deleted automatically within 90 days.

  • Google Sign-In (OIDC / PKCE) in EventWhisperer: Processes your display name, email address, and session tokens solely for authentication. Tokens are encrypted locally on your Android device via Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM) and excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.
  • Calendar Data (READ_CALENDAR): Read strictly locally on your Android device to display events and timers, and never uploaded to Broccoli Buttons servers or AI services.
  • Crash & Stability Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics in test/release builds): Sends TLS-encrypted, redacted crash logs and app/installation identifiers (emails and OAuth tokens scrubbed before upload), automatically deleted within 90 days.
  • Support & Deletion Rights: Support messages to broccolibuttons@gmail.com are used only to help you. Sign out or clear EventWhisperer app storage on Android anytime to delete local tokens, or email us to remove support correspondence.
